DYSTOPIAN INDIA POST THE KALYUG PERIOD

Tools: Adobe Photoshop

In dystopian India, in year 4,30,900, Kalyug is predicted to end, and humans have resorted to worshipping the demon Kali, since they believe humanity must be wiped out and cleansed of all their evil to restart the cycle of birth and purity. This era was marked by the meteor shower Pralaya, which consisted of zero gravity materials.  When placed in any space, the rocks made of the material Levitatium defied the laws of gravity and stayed put in their space. The few humans who were left, harnessed this material to construct the gigantic, floating statue of Kali, with a levitating head made of Levitatium. The rest of the landscape is in ruins and humans are sacrificing each other in the large lava pit under the statue, to put an end to humanity. Will humanity perish once and for all, or is the end of a new beginning?
