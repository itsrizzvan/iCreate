About Me

A designer focused on mechanics that are easy to read, satisfying to master, and memorable long after the screen fades.

I'm Ivan, a game developer and designer who enjoys turning ideas into interactive experiences. I build games in Unity, with a strong focus on clean architecture, thoughtful systems, and technical problem-solving, while also exploring level design, concept art, and 3D modeling. I enjoy working where creativity and technology intersect, bringing ideas to life through both design and development.

Beyond games, I'm passionate about storytelling through writing, worldbuilding, and music. Whether I'm sketching, playing the guitar or ukulele, or working on new stories and creative projects, I'm always exploring new ways to create meaningful experiences and grow as a multidisciplinary creator.

Character Study 1

Superhero

For the superhero assignment, we were tasked with creating 10 superhero masks and the lore behind each mask. This included establishing why the superhero was needed, providing a compelling reason for them to wear a mask beyond simply concealing their identity, and defining the goals they sought to accomplish as masked superheroes.

We also had the option of creating supervillains for the assignment. As a group, we decided to build a shared universe where all 10 superheroes and supervillains could coexist. To give the characters a sense of cohesion, we developed interconnected backstories and a common world that tied each of them together as part of the same universe.

My contribution to this project was the creation of three of the ten masks, including their concepts, backstories, and roles within the shared universe.

Character Study 2

Indianized House of the Dragon Concept Art

For this assignment, we were tasked with creating concept art for two characters from a game or television series of our choice. The process began with selecting a game or series, followed by choosing two characters. We then conducted extensive reference collection for both characters, created black-and-white renders exploring a variety of poses, and experimented with different colour schemes and palettes. After selecting the strongest combination of pose and colour scheme, we produced a final rendered illustration with an optional background.

For our project, we chose House of the Dragon as the source material, with Daemon Targaryen and Caraxes as our selected characters. We reimagined them within the Gupta period of Indian history, adapting their designs to reflect the aesthetics, attire, and cultural influences of that era before following the complete concept art pipeline.

Environment Study 01

Dystopian India in the Age after Kalyug

For this assignment, we were given the creative freedom to design any environment of our choice, with the requirement that it be rooted in Indian culture. We chose to imagine India 6,700 years in the future, at the predicted end of Kalyug, and built a dystopian world around this concept.

As part of the project, I contributed by gathering visual references, exploring and curating mood boards, and helping refine the finer details of the city in the final render to enhance its depth and atmosphere.
